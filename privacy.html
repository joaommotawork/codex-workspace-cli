Privacy policy

Codex Workspace CLI is a personal tool used only by its owner on their own Google account.

  • Google user data is accessed only by the owner, on the owner's own computer, to organise and manage their own account.
  • No data is collected, stored on external servers, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • Credentials are stored encrypted on the owner's device.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of Google user data adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
